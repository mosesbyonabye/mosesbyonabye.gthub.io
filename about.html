Passionate About Electrical Innovation

Hi, I'm Byonabye Moses, an Electrical Engineer dedicated to powering the future through innovative design, renewable energy, and reliable systems.

I design and develop practical solutions across power systems, industrial control panels, embedded motor control, and renewable energy integration turning ideas into dependable engineering. I bridge the gap between complex electrical theory and functional, real-world applications. By focusing on efficiency and sustainability, I ensure that every design aligns with modern environmental goals and strict industrial standards.

A major focus of my work is advancing sustainable infrastructure through comprehensive project feasibility reviews and performance analysis using RETScreen workflows. This analytical approach directly informs my hands-on projects , which include engineering dependable Solar Hybrid Systems, Solar Backup Systems, and managing complete electrical installations in residential homes. I also integrate critical modern infrastructure by managing Computer Networks & Camera Installations for complete system security.

I enjoy mentoring aspiring engineers and collaborating on real-world prototypes where performance, reliability, and clean documentation matter.
